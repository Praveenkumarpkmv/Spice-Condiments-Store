The Workshop Discipline

Four days. Every jar.

❦

Our kitchen runs on an unhurried weekly rhythm. Nothing starts until fresh heirloom produce arrives at dawn on Monday morning.

01 Monday

Harvest & Sorting

Crates arrive from partner growers. Every fruit is washed in well-water, weighed, and logged against its micro-batch ledger number.

Single-estate produce only
02 Tuesday

Toasting & Maceration

Whole spices are toasted on cast-iron griddles, then stoneground coarse. Fruit is layered with organic jaggery to macerate overnight.

Cast-iron gentle roast
03 Wed–Thu

Copper Pot Simmer

Simmered 6 hours in heavy French copper cauldrons with neem paddles. Zero artificial pectin, zero gums — set purely by natural reduction.

Zero thickeners or gums
04 Friday

Wax Seal & Cellar Rest

Poured warm into sterilised glass, sealed with organic beeswax, hand-numbered, and cellared for six weeks before release.

6-week dark cellar rest
0% Artificial Pectin Natural fruit reduction
6 Wks Glass Maturation Cellared in Fort Kochi
100% Direct Farm Sourced 32 single-estate growers
180 Jars Per Run Strict batch control
