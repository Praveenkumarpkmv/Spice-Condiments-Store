The Method

Four days. Every jar.

Our workshop runs on a fixed weekly rhythm. Nothing starts until the produce arrives Monday morning.

Day 01 · Monday

Pick & weigh

Produce arrives early. Every crate is weighed, tagged, and logged against the batch it will become.

Day 02 · Tuesday

Prep & grind

Whole spices are toasted and stone-ground. Fruit is peeled, seeded, and left to macerate overnight.

Day 03–04 · Wed–Thu

Copper pot reduction

Low simmer for four to six hours. No pectin, no gums, no thickeners — just time and a wooden spoon.

Day 05 · Friday

Jar, ink & rest

Sealed and dated by hand. Then the long rest — six weeks in the dark before any jar leaves the workshop.
