Before You Write

Answers to the common questions.

If you've checked below and still need help, please write — we'd rather answer a question twice than have you wonder.

Write to us anyway

Do you ship outside India?

Yes — we ship to 22 countries. International orders take 7–14 days and are packed in shock-resistant cardboard with muslin padding.

How long does a jar keep?

Between four and twelve months once opened, depending on the jar. Every lid has a printed 'best by' date — and the jar's original rest period is on the batch ledger.

Can I visit the workshop without an appointment?

Yes. The workshop is open Tuesday through Sunday, 9 AM to 7 PM. No booking needed — just walk in.

Do you offer wholesale pricing?

Yes — from 200 jars up. Cafés, delis, and gift makers order directly from the workshop. Custom labels available at no extra cost.

What if I don't like a jar I bought?

If the seal is broken on arrival, we replace it — no questions. If you simply don't like the taste, write to us and we'll credit your next order.

— one more way in —

Prefer to write a real letter?

We keep a wooden tray by the workshop door for handwritten notes. Post them to us, or drop them off in person — every letter gets a reply, usually within a week.
