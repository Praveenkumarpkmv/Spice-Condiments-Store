Behind the jar

Every jar comes from a specific field.

Here's how three of our partner farms made it onto the shelf.

Field notes

Nellore

Andhra Pradesh

Nellore, Andhra Pradesh

Chandra Reddy's coriander

Chandra plants coriander every February and harvests in the last week of April. He sun-dries the seeds on the same woven mat his father used and sells us one full sack a season. We use his coriander in three of our blends — you can taste its citrus backbone in the Tomato & Tamarind chutney.

Partner since 2003 1 crop per year

Field notes

Idukki

Kerala · 3,200 ft

Idukki, Kerala

Latha's green cardamom

Latha runs a 3-acre cardamom plot at 3,200 feet. She sends us one hundred kilos every October — enough for our entire winter batch of chai blends. The pods arrive still smelling of the mist that grew them.

Partner since 2011 Altitude grown

Field notes

Alleppey

Kerala · Turmeric country

Alleppey, Kerala

Suresh's Alleppey turmeric

Suresh grows the fattest turmeric roots we've ever seen. Rich colour, high curcumin, and a smell that fills the entire workshop when we grind it. Every Haldi Mustard jar starts with his crop.

Partner since 2008 Organic certified
Buying Notes

Before you open the jar.

Questions we get most often from customers ordering for the first time. If we've missed something, write to us — we answer every letter.

Write to us
i.

How long does a jar keep?

Between four and twelve months once opened, depending on the jar. Every lid has a printed 'best by' date — and the jar's original rest period is on the batch ledger.

ii.

Do you ship outside India?

Yes — we ship to 22 countries. International orders take 7–14 days and are packed in shock-resistant cardboard with muslin padding.

iii.

Can I return a jar?

If a seal is broken on arrival, we replace it — no questions. If you simply don't like the taste, write to us and we'll credit your next order.

iv.

Do you offer wholesale?

Yes — from 200 jars up. Cafés, delis, and gift makers order directly from the workshop. Custom labels available.

The workshop is open Tuesday through Sunday. Walk in, pick a jar off the shelf, and we'll hand you a tasting spoon before you pay.
