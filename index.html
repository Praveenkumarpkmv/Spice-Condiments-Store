From the Source

Every spice has a name, a farm, and a harvest date.

We work directly with 32 smallholder farms — no auctions, no middlemen, no mystery sacks. Each batch we receive is logged with the grower's name, the village, and the week it was sun-dried.

Grown, not sourced

Direct trade agreements with every farm, reviewed each season.

Sun-dried, never kiln-forced

Slow drying preserves volatile oils that carry aroma into your kitchen.

Stone-ground in small batches

We grind weekly, not monthly — so what reaches you is at peak fragrance.

From the Kitchen

Three ways to cook with Garuda this week.

View all recipes

Slow-Cooked Dal Makhani

Black lentils simmered overnight, finished with a spoon of Garuda at the very end to keep its aroma intact.

Spiced Roast Roots

Carrots, beets, and sweet potato tossed in olive oil and a heavy dusting of Garuda before roasting.

Evening Masala Chai

Assam black tea, full-fat milk, and a quarter spoon of Garuda simmered low — our most-brewed house recipe.
